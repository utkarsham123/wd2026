Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a great scientist, teacher, and the 11th President of India. He was known for his simplicity, knowledge, and love for students.

Achievements

  • Known as the Missile Man of India.
  • Played an important role in India's missile and space programs.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna award.

Why He is Inspiring

He inspired young people to dream big, work hard, and serve the nation with honesty and dedication.
